MINECRAFT WORLD — EXPLORER RECIPES v1.0
Bedrock Edition 1.21+ — original behavior pack.

INSTALL
1. Open the .mcaddon file in Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
2. In your world's Behavior Packs settings, activate "Minecraft World | Explorer Recipes".
3. Use any crafting table.
